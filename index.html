THE ROYAL GAME OF UR

Excavated from the Royal Cemetery of Ur (Mesopotamia, c. 2600 BCE), this is one of the oldest board games known to humanity.

The 4 Tetrahedral Dice:
  • Each die is a regular 4-sided pyramid (tetrahedron).
  • 2 corners are marked with white inlaid rosettes (scores 1).
  • 2 corners are blank (scores 0).
  • When rolled, the die rests on a flat face and one corner points directly UP.
  • If the upward-pointing corner is marked, it scores 1; otherwise 0.

Total Roll Probabilities:

• 0 (6.25%) — No moves! Turn is lost.
• 1 (25.0%) — Move 1 square.
• 2 (37.5%) — Move 2 squares.
• 3 (25.0%) — Move 3 squares.
• 4 (6.25%) — Move 4 squares (Max roll!).
